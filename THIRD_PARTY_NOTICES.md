## Generic WebXR hand

`Art/GripHand/SourceHand.glb`, the adapted editable `GripHand.blend`, and the
runtime hand surfaces derive from the generic right-hand model in
[WebXR Input Profiles](https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/tree/main/packages/assets/profiles/generic-hand).
The assets package is distributed under the [MIT license](https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/blob/main/packages/assets/LICENSE.md).

kinematic parenting, reconstructs compatible quads, smooths the posed surfaces,
authors illustrative grip actions, and exports digit masks and evaluated geometry.
