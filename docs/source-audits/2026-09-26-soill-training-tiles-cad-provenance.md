## Verification

The FCStd reopened and recomputed without changing its bytes. The shared
compiler validated the 20-contact inventory, wrote and reopened its USDZ,
derived the descriptor from exported triangles, and validated the staged
package. The generated manifest matched the deleted `board.json` byte for
byte. Android staging produced byte-identical board metadata, USDZ, and
descriptor. The committed USDZ has no material bindings or shader nodes.

The front, side, and top previews of the prior committed asset and compiled
asset are retained for review under
`.context/prolific-ostrich/training-tiles/previews/final/`.

The first candidate reduced each body half from roughly 142,800 to 50,000
triangles. Its sampled two-way diagnostic measured up to 8.70 mm of deviation,
so it was rejected. The final source stores every original body triangle. A
complete, order-independent triangle-connectivity comparison maps each final
vertex to the nearest reference vertex and confirms the same 285,652 body
triangles, with a maximum coordinate delta of 0.000034 mm. All 20 contact
triangle multisets match at 0.01 mm quantization, and their maximum vertex
delta is likewise 0.000034 mm. A nearest-centroid sampled diagnostic gave
spurious 7–9 mm values even for the final identical connectivity because its
128 centroid shortlist can miss a nearby long triangle; it is not used as the
acceptance measurement. The final front, side, and top comparison was visually
reviewed beside the committed asset.
