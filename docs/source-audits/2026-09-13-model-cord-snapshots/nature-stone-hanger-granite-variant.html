Meet the Stone Hanger: the climber's ultimate portable hangboard. Made with FSC Certified oak and Norwegian granite, it offers climbers over 10 grip and pinch positions for comprehensive finger training. Designed by climbers, for climbers who demand both aesthetics and functionality. Sustainable, portable, and unmatched in quality—prepare to take your training to new heights, anywhere.

After spending months getting to the core of climbers' training and warm-up needs, we discovered the crucial role of portable hangboards. Our first version of the Stone Hanger received fantastic feedback, and we were inspired to improve on that. The upgraded Stone Hanger is here: it's stronger, more elegant, and incredibly versatile.

Designed to comfortably support over 100kg, it offers varied training options with 20, 15, 10, and 6mm edges for pinch and crimp exercises. The stone texture is specifically crafted to help prepare your skin and increase pain tolerance for rock climbing.

We believe this improved Stone Hanger is the essential tool for finger strength training, ready to become every climber's best friend.

Angle

ANGLE TYPES

Our holds come in three different angles: sloped, vertical and incut. Sloped holds are the most advanced to climb on, as the 30° angle requires good finger strength and technique. Vertical holds offer a flat surface and are fairly easy to grip, which makes them very versatile in their use on the climbing wall. Incut holds have a 15° angle towards the wall,  offering a more stable grip and thus making them perfect for training on overhangs.

Stone type

GRAY GRANITE / ROSE SANDSTONE

You can choose between two different stone types for your new holds / rails / hangboards. They either come in gray Finnish Granite or in the rose-colored Italian Sandstone. The granite has a naturally rough surface, which is easy to grip and will prepare your hands for outdoor bouldering in Southern Switzerland or Yosemite National Parc. The sandstone is a bit more smooth and great for training on less grippy surfaces like in Fontainebleau and the Red Rock Canyon.

Treatment

RAW WOOD / SHIELDED WOOD

We offer all of our stone woodies in two different surface finishes. We can leave the wood raw, so it stays lighter in color and results in a very natural looking aesthetic and you can profit from the friction of the wood when climbing. Shielding the wood makes the wood richer in color and makes the surface water repellent and removes the friction of the wood. If you plan on washing your holds regularly, we recommend getting shielded climbing holds.

Wooden Sliders

Wooden Sliders

Our sliders are designed as add-ons for our hangboards, allowing you to customize the ledge size for your training sessions by reducing its dimensions.

Each slider boasts a 6 mm (0.25") thickness and is equipped with a magnet on its back, enabling you to easily attach it to the hangboard. Additionally, you have the flexibility to stack two sliders on top of each other, effectively increasing the total thickness to 12 mm (0.5").

 

Engraving

ENGRAVING OPTION

If you want to add a little extra personalization to your brush, we've got you covered! Add up to 25 characters and we will put our laser engraver to work. This is also a great opportunity to add contact details, like phone number, in case your favourite brush gets lost.

Color

COLOR OPTION

To make your climb a bit more colourful, we add a color on our logo on the brushes. You get to choose which one. Can you find your favourite color?
