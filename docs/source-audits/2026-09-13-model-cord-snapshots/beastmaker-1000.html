Beastmaker 1000 Series

Regular price £105.00
/
Tax included. Shipping calculated at checkout.
Only -9295 items in stock!
 
 

The World's most popular fingerboard! 

The Beastmaker 1000 hangboard has been ergonomically designed for the hands of those who are a bit newer to climbing and training but want to progress quickly. This board is suitable for those climbing from 5a (V1) all the way to 7c+ (V10) or even further if you fancy taking on some one armed deadhangs.

The dirty 45 degree slopers and monos which are apparent on the Beastmaker 2000 have been removed to make way for some jugs and more variation of the pocket depths. The Beastmaker 1000 is made from wood with a very fine texture which is kind on your skin and the holds have been nicely rounded to avoid tweaking any fingers. The holds include:

  • 2 Jugs
  • 35 Degree Slopers
  • 20 Degree Slopers
  • 1 Very Deep 4 Finger Pocket
  • 2 Deep 4 Finger Pockets
  • 2 Deep 3 Finger Pockets
  • 2 Deep 2 Finger Pockets
  • 2 Medium 4 Finger Pockets
  • 2 Small 4 Finger Pockets (10mm)
  • 2 Medium 2 Finger Pockets
  • 2 Medium 3 Finger Pockets

Comes with 6 screws for installation (2X70mm,2X60mm,2X50mmm) 

Dimensions - 580mm X 150mm Height X 5mm
