Our fully-featured hangboard.

The Megalith is our hangboard with jugs, pockets, and a variety of flat and unlevel shoulder-width edges.
With an ergonomic full-width pull up jug and edges ranging from 8mm to 40mm, the Megalith will prepare you for any objective.
Bundle the Megalith with the Doorway Mount and we’ll install it for you, so you can get to training immediately.
Train smarter, progress faster, with the Megalith.

The cosmetic defect version is fully functional but imperfect and may have repaired cracks, dents, rough areas, old logo etc.

Product highlights:

Shoulder width edges 8mm-40mm

Full width pull-up bar jug

Incut center hold for one-arm progression
