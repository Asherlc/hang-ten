Baguette Evo - The ultimate mobile hangboard for climbing training and warm-up

Lightweight. Versatile. Comfortable. Portable. The Baguette EVO is the most complete nomadic hangboard for warming up or training without wall mounting.

With 12 technical grips and an innovative Turn & Pull system for infinitely variable inclination, the Baguette Baguette EVO effectively replaces a fixed hangboard , while still fitting in a bag.

Comfortable, lightweight and sturdy, it's ideal for both experienced and expert climbers who want to progress or warm up anywhere, anytime, on the crag, in the gym, in the van or at home.

Baguette Evo

Features

Adaptability

Innovative Turn & Pull system

Thanks to the Turn & Pull mechanism, you can change the angle of the grips in a matter of seconds. This makes each grip more difficult or more accessible, depending on the angle chosen - ideal for adapting effort to your level, whether you're warming up or training.

Ultra-polyvalence

12 grips in an ultra-versatile format

Baguette Evo combines 12 holds in a single, compact piece of equipment. Ranging from 10 to 30 mm, each grip has been designed to meet a specific objective: warm-up, maximum strength, finger endurance or injury prevention.

hangboard mobile

Nomadic format, no installation required

The Baguette Evo can be used on the floor, in a van, outdoors or even indoors, and requires no wall installation. Its flat shape and light weight make it the ideal tool for training anywhere, without constraint.

Smart Product

Comfort and performance

The edges are rounded, the depths carefully worked, the touch soft and non-aggressive. The result: a comfortable grip that respects your fingers and limits pain, even during long or intense sessions.
