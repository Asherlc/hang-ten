Zlagboard.Evo

The Zlagboard.Evo is the latest product in the Zlagboard series and comes in a compact, light design. This board offers versatile training options on crimps, pockets, slopers and jugs, developed for beginner to intermediate climbers and climbers with limited training space.

Zlagboard.Pro

The Zlagboard Pro is the right hangboard for all performance levels, from beginner to professional. This board offers versatile training possibilities and a variety of ergonomical hold shapes and sizes, capable of accompanying climbers throughout their entire climbing career. Developed together with pro climbers and coaches, the Pro aims to maximize training development.
100% quality made in South Tyrol
All Zlagboards are made out of noble lime wood. Both the board itself and the metal components are processed and assembled in collaboration with local businesses in our home region of South Tyrol. The skin-friendly surface of the wood provides  comfort for regular training sessions.

Additional Equipment
Smartphone mount

With the universal smartphone mount, you can quickly fix your phone to the Zlagboard and start training. Thanks to the simple design and flexible material, it's easy to use and compatible with the Zlagboard.Pro, .Evo, .Mini and .Vario.

Slopers

The Slopers were designed for the Zlagboard Pro 2.0 and Zlagboard Evo. They are a perfect additional training tool for improving climbing performance, and are easily mountable on your Zlagboard. The half-spheres are made of solid beechwood and are especially suited to train your open-handed grip strength and improve your body tension.
