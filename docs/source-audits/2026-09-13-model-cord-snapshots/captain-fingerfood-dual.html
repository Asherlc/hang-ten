The fusion of POCKET and UNLEVEL Hangboard. 

The compact hangboard features one straight and one curved edge. This allows the DUAL Hangboard to kill two birds with one stone, enabling versatile and balanced finger strength training with minimal equipment. 


Funktionalität

The joints of our fingers are naturally of different lengths. This results in different angles of the proximal interphalangeal (PIP) joints when gripping a straight handle. The greater the difference in length between the middle and adjacent fingers, the steeper the angle at the middle finger's PIP joint becomes in a half crimp grip. Under load, this creates an uneven distribution of forces, which can lead not only to different force ratios but also, potentially, to overuse injuries and joint capsule problems.

The curved shape of the UNLEVEL Hangboard compensates for the difference in length and distributes the load more evenly to the fingers with identical joint positions.  

The symmetrical grip shape allows you to train both hands on one bar.

Product details

Each grip edge is ergonomically rounded and does not cut uncomfortably into the skin. The grip surfaces have a pleasantly slight indentation under load.

  • Strip depth: 20mm
  • Weight: approx. 150g
  • Total rope length: 1m
