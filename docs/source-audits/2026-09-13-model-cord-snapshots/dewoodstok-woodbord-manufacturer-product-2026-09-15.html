Woodbord
deWoodstok’s woodbord is a hangboard designed for climbers of all levels to increase or maintain finger strength. All holds are nicely rounded, ensuring comfort and reducing the risk of injury for your fingers. Made from certified bamboo, this innovative hangboard combines performance, aesthetic appeal, and sustainability.

Bamboo Benefits:

  • Performance: The fibers of bamboo provide exceptional high friction while being gentle on the skin, making it ideal for effective training.
  • Aesthetic Appeal: The natural color and grain texture of bamboo enhance any home or training space.
  • Sustainability: Bamboo is a renewable resource that grows rapidly—up to 1 meter per day! After harvesting, the plant continues to thrive, eliminating the need for deforestation. Additionally, bamboo absorbs more carbon dioxide than all types of wood. This makes it a great choice for climbers who care about the environment.

Features:

  • A large declining rim on top, perfect for warming up
  • 4-finger pockets with depths of 13, 16, 20, 25, 30, and 35 mm
  • 2-finger pockets with depths of 20 and 35 mm
  • 4 stainless steel screws for wall mounting
