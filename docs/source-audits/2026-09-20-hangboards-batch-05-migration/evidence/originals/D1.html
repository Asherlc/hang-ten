Will the DoorMount Pro fit my doorway?

The DoorMount Pro is designed to fit most doorways, but to ensure it will work for your space, here are the measurements.

Top trim maximum height:

Top trim must be less than 5.5 inches (139.1mm) in height.

Top trim to ceiling clearance:

There must be more than 2.87 inches (73mm) of clearance between the top of the trim and the ceiling.

Top trim maximum height:

Top trim must be less than 4.6 inches (116.6mm) in height.

Top trim to ceiling clearance:

There must be more than 4.5 inches (115mm) of clearance between the top of the trim and the ceiling.

Maximum doorway thickness:

The doorway needs to be less than 9 inches (228.6mm) at its thickest point.

Trim thickness (minimum and maximum)

Trim thickness needs to be between .25 inches (6mm) and 1.2 inches (30mm).

Most doorways are structural and will support the DoorMount Pro.

The DoorMount Pro uses your door trim only for positioning and alignment, not for bearing weight. The holding power comes from tightening the thumb knob, which compresses the mount against the wall above the doorway. This creates a rigid clamping force into the structural framing (the header and studs) behind the drywall.

If there is any movement, just tighten the thumb knob until the board feels like it's bolted into the wall.
