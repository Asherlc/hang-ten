## Conflict rulings

**Manufacturer dimension axes not explicit** — Interpret 12.75 inches as per-half width and 5.25 as height from the front aspect and individual-half listing. Thickness remains estimated.

**Photo shows five openings per half; manual supplies four screws per half** — Retain all five observed openings per half. Classify four small openings as mounting holes and the larger IMR opening as an observed passage with unresolved purpose; do not call it a hold or cord attachment.

**Pinch/IMR grip labels overlap physically** — Keep one IMR contact per half; medium/narrow pinch patches are reused in a wide-pinch interaction group. Do not clone the IM/IMR surfaces.
