# Hangboard warm-up source review

Primary sources checked October 30, 2026. The four previous standalone guides
were removed with their unreferenced blocks: Lattice progressive, Beastmaker
general, Climbing boulder pyramid, and California Climbing Collective rope
practice. Existing workout warm-up sections are unchanged.

A future standalone warm-up must prescribe concrete steps performed on a
hangboard. General preparation advice and wall sessions do not qualify.
The sources below are research candidates, not imported catalog routines.

## PitchSix / Collin McGee — Recruitment Prep

[Manufacturer protocol](https://pitchsix.com/blogs/blog/the-best-hangboarding-warm-up),
“Warmup 1: Recruitment Prep”.

Six sets, each two five-second pulls: finger-driven overcoming isometrics at
50%, 70%, 80%, then 90% of the corresponding maximum; yielding isometrics at
60%, then 80%. The source specifies a slightly open half crimp, ideally on an
uneven edge of at least 25 mm; perceived effort is an allowed alternative.

This is a portable-edge / Force Board warm-up. The article does not supply
inter-repetition or inter-set rests. Inspect its linked video and app evidence
before importing; do not invent rests or equate finger squeezing with hanging.
Its separate Endurance Prep should follow Recruitment Prep, rather than being
presented as an independent complete warm-up.

## Entralpi — ramp before max hangs

[Manufacturer guidance](https://entralpi.com/help/train/plan-your-training/warm-up-routine),
“How do you ramp up before max hangs?”.

Two to three progressively harder hangs on the intended training edge and grip:
approximately 50%, 70%, then 85% maximum; each lasts 5–10 seconds with 1–2 minutes
between efforts. This is a specific preparation sequence after lighter work,
not the page's entire warm-up. The author labels it coaching practice, not a
trial-validated protocol. Preserve those qualifications and ranges if imported.

## TrainingBeta — checked, weaker fit

[Author-published guide](https://www.trainingbeta.com/warming-hangboard-session/),
embedded page “Warming up on the hangboard”
([source image](https://www.trainingbeta.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/hangboardwarmup.jpg)).

Starts with twenty-second jug hangs and progressively smaller non-limit holds,
repeating twenty-second hangs over 15–20 minutes until warm. Also includes
arm circles, wrist stretches and finger flicks. No fixed repetition or rest
sequence is supplied, and the routine includes off-board activity. It is a
weaker match than the two candidates above.

## Import boundary

No replacement has been added by this research change. A catalog import still
needs a field-by-field mapping, supported hand targets and action semantics,
honest provenance, validation and app review under `ADDING_A_ROUTINE.md`.
