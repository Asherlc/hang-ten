### Superseded Crimptonite Helium Mobile candidates

https://crimptonite.com/product/helium-mobile/ mixes two distinct boards. The
current Helium Mobile is the three-cavity portable strip shown in `DSCF6543`
and `DSCF6544`: each cavity has opposing engraved depths, with 22/14 mm on the
outer cavities and 18/10 mm on the center cavity. That topology agrees with the
