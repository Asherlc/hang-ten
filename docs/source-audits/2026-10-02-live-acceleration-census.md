# Within-step acceleration eligibility census

A depth-two Anderson proposal needs three map evaluations to form history,
restored lengths and a fresh ordinary QP at the accelerated state. The fixed
540-step current-source trajectory contains 1,657 corrections; 317 steps need
only two corrections. Even ignoring every signature, at most 157 corrections
could disappear under that evaluation schedule. Requiring three consecutive
identical row and active counts leaves only one optimistically removable
correction, at step 101. Counts are merely a necessary condition, not proof of
full feature or active-set identity.

This closes the mechanism before building provenance tracking or proposing
states. The count is not a wall-time bound. No physical gate, model, activation
or threshold changed, and no candidate or product code was implemented. The
adjacent JSON binds the read-only script and complete retained input.
