# Triangle feature agreement census

Instrumentation retains all original endpoint, ray and three edge calculations,
tracking diagnostic triangleClosest feature labels and original final per-face
row/merit winner IDs. Worker slots are disjoint and combined after joins. Every
139-prefix and seven step-140 complete checkpoint and correction schedule
matched the independent current source, with two corrections and zero caps/retries.

All 14,934 faces are eligible. Agreement counts are 558/726/864 at vertices,
2,766/3,033/5,613 on edges and 636 on the face. Keeping both incident edges at
vertices leaves 26,880 optimistic omitted calls out of 44,802, **0.599973**.
No original winner belongs to a proposed omitted edge. This nevertheless fails
the registered **≥0.60** coverage gate and closes this experiment before a
Voronoi proof or candidate. It is not rounded to a pass. The miss is not a
mathematical refutation or a wall-time bound; the screen counts opportunities
without certifying floating branches, rounding, ties or runtime cost.

Primary research inspected the author's [segment-triangle query](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/davideberly/GeometricTools/master/GTE/Mathematics/DistSegment3Triangle3.h)
and [line-triangle query](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/davideberly/GeometricTools/master/GTE/Mathematics/DistLine3Triangle3.h).
These generic implementations compare edges; they establish no speed result
for this model. No external code was incorporated.

The first compile reused a top-level variable name and ran no measurement;
the corrected build is separate retained evidence. The adjacent JSON binds
source, plan, results and three verified deleted child groups. No product code,
query shortcut, physical gate or runtime claim changed.
