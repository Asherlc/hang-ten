# Warm-up guides and self-paced execution

Primary sources checked October 27, 2026. All four guides are board-flexible:
none refers to a numbered hold inventory. They are standalone **adaptations**,
with `category: warmup`, `focus: warmUp`, and `workoutLabels: [warm-up]`.
“Warm-up” in the level field is a purpose label, not an invented difficulty
assessment. The titles identify the source; subtitles describe the retained
activity, counts and equipment. No guide is appended to another workout.

## Lattice — `warmup.lattice.progressive`

[Ollie Torr, Finger Strength Training for Climbers](https://www.latticetraining.com/blog/how-to-manage-finger-strength-for-climbers),
section “Build up during every session”.

| Authored field / step | Source fact and adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| `warmup.lattice.build` title, instruction, accessory | Build gradually to working load over 3–4 sets; do not begin at working load. |
| Timing, grip, holds, hands, rest, load | The warm-up paragraph specifies none. This is one self-paced instruction, with no invented per-set timer, grip or hand target. |
| Standalone plan / provenance | Extracts preparation guidance from a larger strength article; not a complete Lattice strength protocol. |

## Ned Feehally — `warmup.beastmaker.general`

[Finger Board Training, hosted by Beacon Climbing](https://www.beaconclimbing.com/wp-content/uploads/Beastmaker-Beacon-Training-Manual.pdf),
PDF page 3, “Warming up”. This is the author’s manual, not a retailer’s
transcription. The paragraph precedes the board-specific training tables.

| Authored field / step | Source fact and adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| `warmup.beastmaker.minimum` | At least 15 minutes on a climbing wall or fingerboard; take time to warm up. The first timer is 900 seconds. |
| `warmup.beastmaker.ready` | Source requires fully warming up before intense finger training. A self-paced continuation after the minimum prevents the timer from declaring the athlete ready. |
| Holds, grips and exercise sequence | Unspecified in the warm-up paragraph; omitted. The subsequent repeater training tables are not imported into the warm-up. |

## Climbing — `warmup.climbing.bouldering`

[Warm-up Pyramid](https://www.climbing.com/skills/warm-up-pyramid/),
sections 1–4. This adaptation selects the bouldering branch and requires a
climbing wall. Sport-climbing grade examples and the 15-minute sport-climbing
final rest are not part of this bouldering guide.

| Step suffix | Prescription retained |
| --- | --- |
| `cardio` | 10 minutes walking, biking or jogging; 600-second timer. |
| `neck` | Five slow head circles each direction; no exercise duration given. |
| `shoulders` | Five controlled arm circles each direction, one arm at a time, then smaller circles in both directions with both arms; no invented small-circle count. |
| `twists` | At least five twists each side, one-minute rest, repeat. Retained together as a self-paced task, including the rest instruction. |
| `lunges` | About 10 walking lunges per leg; self-paced. |
| `easy` | Several easy boulders; reduce grade if strenuous or pumpy; self-paced. |
| `rest-1` | About five minutes; five-minute timer is an explicitly disclosed selection of the approximate source duration. |
| `moderate` | A few problems two to three grades below the athlete’s limit; self-paced. |
| `depump` | A few minutes until recovered; manual rest, no invented fixed duration. |
| `final` | A below-limit climb resembling the project’s style; self-paced. |
| `rest-final` | 10-minute final rest for bouldering; narrowly allowlisted as source-required terminal rest. |

Titles and accessories are concise task summaries. No source physiological or
injury-prevention claim is added to the coaching text. Grade-specific examples
are omitted in favor of the source’s relative difficulty instructions.

## California Climbing Collective — `warmup.ccc.rope-practice`

[Rope Season ISO Warm-up Checklist](https://www.californiaclimbingcollective.com/ropewarmup),
phases 1–3. Selects **practice**, not the longer competition windows.

| Step | Source facts and app representation |
| --- | --- |
| `phase-1` | 20-minute practice window, half cardio/mobility and half easy climbing. Preserves the listed cardio counts (100 rope, 3 × 7 burpees, 50 high knees, 3 × 10 jump squats), five-minute mobility guidance and named movements, 10-minute easy climbing and 10–15-move sequences, footwork/breathing and light-pump guidance. |
| `phase-2` | 15-minute practice window; 5–7-move sequences over varied holds, intentional hands/thumbs, moderate movement variety, 2–4-minute circuit or four up-down-ups, appropriate pump without exhaustion. |
| `phase-3` | 15-minute practice window; 5–10-move crux sequences of simple hard and complex movements, generous rest, avoiding fatigue, pushing and pulling. |

The three phase timers are 1200/900/900 seconds. Their internal tasks are
instruction checklists, not invented individually timed exercises. The guide
summarizes the checklist rather than claiming every example is a distinct
prescribed interval. It requires a climbing wall; no board contact or finger
configuration is inferred from the selected hangboard.

## Timing and recording contract

An omitted enclosing `duration` decodes as zero and is valid only for an
untimed conditioning instruction or one explicitly nonfixed segment. Fixed
timing still requires a positive valid enclosing duration. An untimed rest
must explicitly use `undefined` timing and no segment duration. A positive
enclosing source cycle still advances at its stated boundary even if its work
duration is unspecified; existing catalog prescriptions remain unchanged.

The workout reserves one internal navigation coordinate for each manual step.
This is not a second of prescribed or observed activity. The clock stops at
its start until explicit Next step / Finish. Pause, interruption, resume,
selection and delayed frames retain that barrier. Explicit completion marks
the navigation slot in the execution ledger; unknown segment duration stays
absent from recorded work/rest. Conditioning instructions have no board-work
target and contribute no invented board-contact activity. Summary rows retain
their instructions and session wall-clock dates.

Plan and flow duration labels say Self-paced, or show timed portions plus
self-paced. Unknown total durations are excluded from numeric duration filters.
There is no final-seconds countdown during an untimed step or across its
boundary. Stopwatch work without an enclosing timer still keeps its independent
measurement control and waits for explicit step advancement.
