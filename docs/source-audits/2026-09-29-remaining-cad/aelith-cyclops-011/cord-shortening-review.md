# Cyclops shorter display cord — 2026-09-30

The user reviewing board 1 of 21 requested: “cord is way too long.” This revision changes only the cord display estimates and native-solver caches in `suspension.json`. The retained manufacturer evidence, one doubled loop through the actual tip eye, terminal stations, native body, radius, camera, and rotation are unchanged.

The loop estimate changes from 360 to 140 mm and the upward display support offset from 100 to 25 mm. Native settling changes the vertical support-to-eye-midpoint span from 167.521849 to 56.478332 mm (33.714% of the prior span). The corresponding straight-line spans are 167.551575 and 56.566442 mm. Free overhead height above the board's native top is 163.473921 → 52.430404 mm; the board is 53.892595 mm tall. These are user-requested **display estimates**, not manufacturer cord dimensions or supplied-accessory claims.

Continuous signed-distance clearance has a certified lower bound of 1.040223 mm for the unchanged 1 mm cord radius (10 µm numerical tolerance). Full route/tube self-intersection validation and package parsing pass.
