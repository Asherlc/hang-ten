Description

The Cyclops portable hangboard from Aelith Equipment is the ideal everyday carry (EDC) training device for climbers of all levels. Featuring a 20mm mono edge, the globally recognized standard, the Cyclops allows you to train various grip types anywhere, anytime. Its compact design easily fits in your pocket, purse, backpack, or clipped to your keys.

Train Anywhere with the 20mm Edge

The 20mm edge supports a wide range of training plans focused on finger strength for crimps, pockets, and monos. Whether you’re warming up at the crag or building strength at home, the Cyclops delivers consistent, effective training in a portable form.

Fully Standalone Design for On-the-Go Training

Designed as a standalone device, the Cyclops requires no additional equipment. It includes a durable, uniquely-designed 550-cord lanyard, available in a wide array of colors, making the Cyclops the perfect EDC companion for the modern climber.

  • Foot Loop: Loop the lanyard over your foot to isolate one finger at a time.

  • Weighted Training: Girth hitch it to a kettlebell, dumbbell or loading pin for weighted pulls.

  • Extra Compatibility: A carabiner hole near the pulling edge allows attachment to cable stacks and other systems.

Aerospace-Grade Durability and Precision

The body of the Cyclops is constructed from precision-machined, aerospace-grade 6061-T6 aluminum, chosen for its unmatched durability, strength and hardness. Unlike wood or plastic, this material resists denting and chipping, allowing the Cyclops to withstand years’ worth of trips to the crag. All of Aelith Equipment’s products, including the Cyclops’ body, are machined using a Langmuir MR-1 Gantry Mill — a precision 3-axis vertical CNC milling machine built for high rigidity, tight tolerances and industrial-level precision.

Anodized for Extra Protection

Aelith developed a skin-friendly surface texture to counter the hardness of aluminum, offering excellent grip with or without chalk. The result is a hangboard that feels gentle on your skin, stays clean, and maintains consistent performance session after session.

Each Cyclops also features a hard anodized coating for superior corrosion resistance and vibrant color. This extra protection prevents common issues like flaking, staining, or pitting, ensuring the Cyclops stands up to rain, sweat, and daily wear.

Unlike raw aluminum, this finish prevents issues like:

  • Streaking

  • Staining

  • Flaking

  • Pitting

Whether you’re training outdoors or indoors, your Cyclops stays clean, strong, and sharp.

Recyclable, Responsible, and Ready for Years

Thanks to its unique aluminum construction, the Cyclops is infinitely recyclable. After years of use, building finger strength, the aluminum body can be recycled into new products—again and again—without any loss in its quality or material properties. This means less waste, less damage to the environment, and a more sustainable approach to climbing gear.

Ergonomics That Matter

The Cyclops was designed with meticulously sculpted ergonomics. Every curve was designed with care and great attention to detail to ensure climbers can comfortably and safely train in full crimp, half crimp and drag positions, without creating pressure points or hotspots. The gently, gradually sloped sides allow for easy grip changes with one hand, while the smooth, flowing form delivers both comfort and aesthetic appeal.

The Cyclops is more than a tool—it’s a piece of art and a reflection of your climbing identity. Shop the Cyclops today.
