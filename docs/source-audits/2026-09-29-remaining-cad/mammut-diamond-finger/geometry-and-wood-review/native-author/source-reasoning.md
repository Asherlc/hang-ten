# Mammut Diamond Finger native revision

This candidate follows the approved exact-revision Mammut product photograph and manual, plus the independent Opus 5.5 review. It is directly authored CAD; no raster coordinates, contours, masks, crops, registration, or geometry measurements were extracted from imagery.

Primary evidence: [product photograph](https://static.mammut.com/master/2060-00020-7458_main_75743.jpg), [manufacturer manual](https://static.mammut.com/file/2060-00020_man_en_070420_DiamondFingerHangboard_Manual.pdf), and [manufacturer press document](https://cdn.uc.assets.prezly.com/da46d83b-e3f0-484b-b349-2047c1a0da6c/-/inline/no/202003_Mammut_DiamondFingerHangboard.docx). The press document independently states 85 cm width and walnut; its embedded product image repeats the existing front photograph and adds no side-angle geometry. Image/manual originals and the press document are retained by root. No new measured thickness, pocket depth, or grip prescription is asserted.

The photograph and manual support a machined form with flat terraces, straight angled transitions, small corner radii, narrower upper ends, and three lower lobes. The previous global smooth side extrusion made those terraces into rounded continuous ridges. The replacement uses flat-faced ruled native lofts, a chamfered flat main rail, separate mirrored outer jug shoulders and separate open end-bay cuts. The central top is visibly lower than the outer jugs and has straight transition facets.

The silhouette now uses straight outward upper side slopes and inward lower slopes. Lower notches have straight sides and flat roofs; the central lobe is narrower. Lower fronts remain upright at a constant front plane instead of the previous unsupported deep scoops and backward taper. The lower central tray has a straight top, angled ends and a central flat-bottom dip. Lateral ledges have a flat inner shelf, diagonal step and flat outer shelf. Analytic stadium profiles replace rounded rectangles for the mono and lower slots. Mono depth 42 mm is a display estimate chosen to give a visibly deeper blind pocket; no through-hole or hidden feature is implied.

Width 850 mm is manufacturer-supported. Height 196 mm, maximum thickness 78 mm, main front depth 62 mm, local bay floor 34 mm behind the rear datum, pocket dimensions/depths, corner radii and bevel widths are operator-selected display estimates. The exact authored coordinates and scalar values are retained in author_candidate.py and authored-design.json. They are reviewable display choices rather than manufacturing dimensions. The native-source conservative bounding-box overrun is explicitly separated from the actual 850 × 78 × 196 mm tessellated extent.

All 16 existing contact IDs and the exact embedded current manifest are preserved. Two visible lower recesses remain physical geometry without adding contact IDs. The old subtitle/inventory discrepancy is not resolved by inventing grips. Phone cradle, mounting hardware, logos and colored inserts remain omitted. No materials/textures are added to CAD or USDZ; root owns the separate runtime wood appearance request.

The saved source contains only native Sketcher/Part features and native face binders. Right-side features feed explicit native Part::Mirroring counterparts. Construction scripts are scratch provenance, not source dependencies. The actual current compiled USDZ c79b5561c618bf7f1f819218d325db54ddf59de38a5000b0e42f5131e0457e54 supplies the before mesh. Previews execute the existing preview.py projection/render functions unchanged on that mesh and on native candidate tessellation; oblique uses a documented rigid view rotation. These are diagnostic native comparisons before official USDZ compilation and app review.

Only workspace-owned scratch paths were written. No servers, simulators, external resources, production package files, locks, or Git state were changed by this authoring lane. Root owns promotion after independent visual review.

## Revision 2 response to the independent second review

The first candidate and its original diagnostics remain under `history/revision-1/`. Revision 2 uses smaller 42 × 22 mm horizontal stadium mono openings and retains their estimated 42 mm blind depth. The lower tray and lateral shelves now have deliberately tapered walls and tilted native floors. These changes make the floor/wall separation visible without increasing the maximum local recess depth beyond the first candidate's approximately 35–36 mm. At native X=0, the tray floor lies 26.71 mm behind the local front near its upper portion and 33.44 mm behind it in the lower dip. Native lateral shelf probes show 30.06–34.47 mm local recess. These are CAD display dimensions, not manufacturer measurements.

Native Part fillets of 0.8 mm soften the blank front perimeter, main rail lip and outer jug shoulders; the mirrored left shoulder inherits the right feature. The central top already slopes down 16 mm across its 62 mm depth, and now has a small rounded front edge. Broad surfaces remain planar or straight chamfers. The native source retains separate inset-step recesses in the outer upper pockets. The small extreme-end steps are deliberate end-bay/shoulder intersections; no new functional groove is claimed.

`native-verification.json` records one valid solid, 39 fully constrained sketches, all 16 contacts on the final wood boundary, no positive-area contact overlap, zero bilateral symmetric-difference volume and exact embedded-manifest preservation. Editing mono depth from 42 to 44 mm changes both mirrored mono contacts through native dependencies; restoring it yields zero material difference in both directions. Numerical volume integration differs by only 1.75e-5 cubic mm after restore. The saved source remains byte-identical through this scratch edit/restore verification.

`section-diagnostic.json` and the native section diagram show the actual cut boundaries. Twelve first-hit rays compare the saved native solid with its diagnostic tessellation, including central/lateral floors and five upper mono probes; maximum disagreement is 4.32e-9 mm. This demonstrates that the sampled openings have real recessed floors and no accidental front caps. It is a sampled geometry check, not a claim that every possible raster artifact has been excluded.

The current oblique comparison uses 18 degrees of positive X rotation and minus 12 degrees about Z, exposing the board from above. The raking comparison uses 30 degrees about X and the same Z rotation. Both old and new assets receive the same transform. An earlier diagnostic looked from below and obscured shelf floors; it is superseded by these current views. The section diagram is independent of raster shading. All comparisons remain provisional native diagnostics pending official export and app review.
