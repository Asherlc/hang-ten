Safety Notice

Climbing training tools place high load on fingers, elbows, and shoulders. Please remember:

  • Never train through sharp pain.
  • Rest between sets and reps.
  • Make sure you have enough friction when performing the pull.

If you are unsure how to train safely, consult a professional coach.
