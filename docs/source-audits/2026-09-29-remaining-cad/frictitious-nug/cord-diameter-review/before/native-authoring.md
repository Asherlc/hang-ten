# The NUG — native CAD source audit

Published 130×60×40 mm; 20/25/8/13 mm edges, 40 mm jug, 60 mm pinch. Front and reverse each contain one stepped cavity.

Body outline corner 18 mm and outer round 3 mm; cavity 88×32 mm corner 7 mm, rim round 2 mm. Front upper 20/lower 25 and reverse upper 8/lower 13 are exact source depths. Mouth-well radius 4/depth 4 mm at x ±53, z 0. All unlisted outline, radius, cavity spacing and mouth dimensions are display estimates.

All six contact records preserved byte-equivalent as structured JSON. Pinch 60 describes opposing span along native z; compiler front-depth metric clamps it to 40 mm and does not reinterpret this as a 40 mm source fact.

The FCStd is the self-contained geometry and metadata authority: constrained Sketcher profiles, analytic Part extrusions/lofts, cuts/fusions/fillets, and native face SubShapeBinders. No imported facet solids, textures, materials, hardware, image tracing or geometry-generation scripts are retained. This is a display model; estimated dimensions are not manufacturing specifications. The schema label `native-parametric-measured-profile` identifies the supported native source type; these raster migrations did not measure an old display mesh.
