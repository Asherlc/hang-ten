Compact, lightweight, and portable finger training.

The Nug covers all the essentials for portable finger training, with a jug, pinch, and four edges, in the smallest form possible.

This tool was designed using information garnered from hundreds of conversations with professional climbers to create the best solution to common hangboarding problems.

Elevate your set up with the sling and carabiner to attach The Nug to weights, your foot, or a Tindeq.

Use The Nug at the crag, in the gym, at home, or in the van.

Lightweight and portable

Train anywhere with The Nug. The compact size makes The Nug great for hiking to the crag and warming up outside, throwing it in your suitcase when traveling, and taking it to the gym for a consistent training tool.

Jug, pinch, and edges

The Nug has a 40mm jug, 60mm pinch, and four edges that are 8mm, 13mm, 20mm, and 25mm, making it suitable for any training need.

All the training essentials

Because of its size, weight, portability, and variety of edges and grips, The Nug has all the essentials training, no matter where you are. The Nug is convenient and reliable for climbers, travelers, and finger strength enthusiasts.
