## Task 2 — analytic model and deliberate visual review

The Astra geometry author reviewed retained attachments 1–4 directly and
authored the model analytically on 2026-09-20. No photograph was read by an
authoring script; no tracing, segmentation, contours, registration, cropping,
pixel measurements, or inferred source dimensions were used. The generated
64 × 64 material image is an analytic color field, not source photography.

The saved source contains exactly one closed, connected body and two deliberate
horizontal contact patches. The body has one front-opening rounded recess,
opposing 16 mm and 25 mm ledges, a stepped recess back, and two centered
through-bores. Both physical ledges remain in the body; only their straight
horizontal surface patches are selectable. Rounded side transitions, recess
walls, bore walls and the rest of the block are body geometry. Euler
characteristic `-2`, manifold-edge checks, connected-component checks, and
through-axis ray checks establish one solid body with two through-handles.

Native Blender coordinates are `+X` right, `+Z` up, front `-Y`. The compiler
maps `(X, Y, Z)` to board `(X, Z, -Y)`. Native spans are
`[0.105, 0.037, 0.077]` m; importer/descriptor spans are
`[0.104999996, 0.077, 0.037]` m, within 1 µm of the sourced envelope.
The contact native-Y and board-Z spans are exactly 16 mm and 25 mm within
the same tolerance. The baseline has the 25 mm ledge down. The 16 mm pose
uses the exact board quaternion `[0, 0, 1, 0]`; the baseline remains
`[0, 0, 0, 1]`. This is a native `-Y` half-turn, so the one front face
continues facing the athlete.
