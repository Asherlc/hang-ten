# Metolius board-package evidence audit

Checked 2026-08-12. This historical audit preserves the official Metolius
sources reviewed for four models. The old incomplete package art was removed.
Future flat packages must be created complete and their canonical paths directly
authored from this primary evidence under `docs/ADDING_A_BOARD.md`.

The Metolius material below labels visible hold groups and, where stated, depth
and pocket-finger counts. Official diagrams and imagery support direct
boundaries; optional capacity, posture, and feature values remain omitted unless
the model-specific source establishes them.
In particular, the Wood Grips Compact II diagram and manual are model-specific
and were not used for any conclusion here.

## Current authoring interpretation

The Contact, Project, and Simulator diagrams can be combined with their official
front views to freeze their symmetric physical inventories. Climbers Edge uses
its product list plus official front/oblique imagery. Do not borrow Compact II
facts across models. For each package, author visible boundaries directly,
mirror only verified pairs, omit unsupported optional facts, validate the flat
package, and visually review every path.
