The product pages establish identity, dimensions, material, named hold
families, published measurements, and accessory state. The front images map
each engraved measurement to a physical recess and establish the visible
contact boundaries. The detail images distinguish the separately bounded top
slopers from the recessed contacts and corroborate rounded recess walls.

The official comparison table's `Grips` values (Light 7, First 10, One 15,
Evo 19) describe different grip offerings, not a count of selectable physical
left/right contact regions. YY Vertical's [official range
article](https://www.yyvertical.com/en/blogs/news/la-nouvelle-gamme-de-poutres-d-escalade-verticalboard)
likewise calls the First's value “10 different grips.” The package inventory
therefore follows the repository's continuous-contact rule: mirrored physical
contacts are separate, and each separately bounded top surface is separate
from the recess below it.

## Frozen physical inventories

### `yy-verticalboard-light`

Twelve contacts: mirrored top jugs; mirrored 30-degree slopers; one center
20-degree sloper; mirrored 20 mm, 45 mm, and 25 mm edges; and one center 40 mm
edge/elastic notch. The product page supplies `54 × 9 × 5 cm`, the three edge
depths, the center 40 mm value, the two sloper angles, and two jugs. The
straight-on image supplies the one-to-one physical placement. The board is
officially symmetric, so right-side paths are exact mirrors.

### `yy-verticalboard-first`

Seventeen contacts: mirrored top jugs; mirrored 35-degree slopers; one center
20-degree sloper; mirrored 25 mm, 45 mm, 33 mm, 20 mm, and 22 mm edges; one
center 40 mm edge; and one center 24 mm edge/elastic notch. The product page
supplies `54 × 13 × 5 cm`, two jugs, 35- and 20-degree slopers, and the paired
edge families. The official comparison table and engraving map the center
40 mm and 24 mm contacts. The board is officially symmetric, so right-side
paths are exact mirrors.

### `yy-verticalboard-one`

Twenty contacts: mirrored top jugs; mirrored 35-degree slopers; one center
20-degree sloper; mirrored 25 mm and 45 mm edges; mirrored 50 mm and 30 mm
two-finger pockets; mirrored inclined 25 mm contacts; one center
multifunction handle; and mirrored 20 mm and 18 mm edges. YY Vertical supplies
`62 × 13 × 5.5 cm`, the two-finger capacity, the 25 mm / 30-degree inclined
family, sloper angles, and included magnetic inserts. The selected official
front view shows the board without inserts installed, so engraved base depths
are retained and insert-adjusted alternatives are not modeled. The board is
officially symmetric, so right-side paths are exact mirrors.

### `yy-verticalboard-evo`

Twenty-five contacts: two top jugs; mirrored 43-degree and 38-degree slopers;
one center 30-degree sloper; two 25 mm edges; two outer 20 mm edges; two
inclined 25 mm two-finger pockets; two upper mono pockets; two 20 mm
two-finger pockets; one center 40 mm edge; one center one-arm handle; two
inclined 30 mm contacts; two lower mono pockets; and two 18 mm edges. The
product page supplies `65 × 14 × 5.5 cm`, capacity for mono and two-finger
families, and included -10 mm inserts. The selected front view has no inserts
installed, so it represents the engraved base contacts. YY Vertical marks the
Evo asymmetric; each side was therefore authored independently rather than
forced into exact mirroring.

## Conflicts and omissions

- Evo's current product technical list and front engraving identify the center
  top sloper as 30 degrees, while the comparison table says 35 degrees. The
  contact is named from the agreeing product list and image; no numeric angle
  field exists in the package schema.
- First's product-page Characteristics copy describes the lateral slopers as
  25 degrees, while the Technical details, official comparison table, and hero
  engraving all identify them as 35 degrees. The package uses 35 degrees
  because those agreeing structured and image sources prevail over the lone
  conflicting summary statement.
- The First and One product-copy summaries omit some values engraved in the
  current official front images. A size is retained only where the engraving
  and comparison table agree on the same physical location.
- Magnetic inserts are accessories/configuration choices, not extra contacts.
  Adjusted depths are not added as duplicate holds.
- No unsupported grip posture, feature tags, capacity on edges, or coaching
  semantics are included. Mono depth in phalanges is not converted to
  millimeters.

## Geometry and asset decisions

Each `primary.png` is a lossless PNG re-encoding of the current official hero
image without crop, registration, geometric alteration, or cleanup. Geometry
was directly authored against those source views. Regular recessed openings
use operator-selected rounded-rectangle, pill, or circle constraints; the
saved paths remain the sole render/highlight/hit-test source. Top jugs and
slopers use directly authored freeform surface paths. No constraint was
inferred from pixels, and no image-driven detection, segmentation, mask,
contour, registration, vectorization, proposal, or cleanup process was used.

All 52 constrained pieces pass the production `+1 px` resize invariants. A
zero-distance save can reserialize decimal precision, so they are verified for
mathematical primitive consistency and no visible snap rather than claimed as
byte-exact.

### Coverage and deliberate blanks

The ledger certifies 119 kinds, 80 scalar depths, 18 finger capacities, two
one-hand capacities, 24 grip enums, and 31 feature arrays. All other outcomes
are explicit source-specific blanks. No YY source gives a depth interval for
one canonical contact. Degree values, mono depth in phalanges, magnetic-insert
states, and Turn & Pull inclination are not millimetre depth or depth ranges.
Non-pocket contacts have `fingerCapacity: notApplicable`; no width-based hand
capacity or edge posture was inferred.

The source conflicts are retained rather than hidden: First's summary says 25°
for lateral slopers while its technical table, comparison table, and front say
35°; Evo's comparison table says 35° for the center sloper while the product
technical list/front say 30°; Evo summary prose says 30 mm for the inclined
bidoigt while the technical list/table/front map it to 25 mm; and Penta Evo's
title says eight grips while two detailed lists enumerate seven per unit. The
more specific agreeing mappings control. The schema has no angle field, so the
angle wording conflicts do not create optional numeric metadata.
