# Texture manifest

Access/research date: 2026-09-11. Every runtime texture is an original neutral approximation generated for this asset; no third-party source image is used as a texture.

| Filename | Resolution | Color space | Assigned material | Origin | License | Evidence status |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| `textures/oak-neutral-basecolor.png` | 1024 × 1024 RGB | sRGB | `Oak \| neutral matte` | Original uniform warm oak-like base color, generated by `scripts/make_textures.py` | CC0-1.0, original file | Neutral approximation. Natural oak and warm appearance supported by current manufacturer photos M01–M04; no grain or coating is asserted |
| `textures/granite-neutral-basecolor.png` | 1024 × 1024 RGB | sRGB | `Granite \| neutral matte` | Original seeded isotropic gray variation from the same script | CC0-1.0, original file | Neutral approximation. Gray granular stone supported by M01/M04; pattern and numeric colors are original, not a specific specimen reconstruction |

Both maps are packed into the `.blend` and embedded in `.glb`. External source PNGs are included unchanged. There are no other texture maps. Material roughness is a scalar approximation: oak 0.73, granite 0.86; metallic is zero. Numerical BRDF values have not been measured from the product.

UVs are unwrapped on the assembled oak surface before the hold partitions are split, preserving alignment at contact boundaries. The granite insert has its own UV islands. The oak texture is uniform; the granite pattern is statistically uniform and nondirectional, so it carries no false grain alignment or source lighting. UV triangles are checked for collapsed area. The material close-up is `renders/05-neutral-material-closeup.png`.

No logo, label artwork, engraving, printed marking, proprietary surface graphic, source photograph pixels, lighting, shadow, background, reflection, normal map or AO is incorporated. Third-party reference images in `evidence/` are research evidence only and have no established app-redistribution license. The original maps may be shipped; the evidence folder should be excluded from runtime distribution.
